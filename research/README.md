# Research records

Tracked files in this directory are the reviewed research truth. Raw downloads,
subscription images, and the structured Ancestry acquisition cache stay local
under ignored paths.

The first intake pass should establish:

1. The deceased starting person or couple.
2. Approximate dates and locations.
3. Known spouses, parents, and children.
4. Which facts came from family knowledge versus a record.
5. The first few questions whose answers would move the tree.

Unknown relationships remain explicit gaps. Conflicting identities remain
separate candidates until evidence supports a resolution.
