# Joseph Roque: Canary Islands candidate rejected

## Why the record mattered

Luís de Rivera’s 1811 burial entry names his parents as Roque and Marciana
Delgado.
An 8 June 1797 Humacao burial then surfaced for a man recorded as Joseph
Roque, explicitly described as a native of the Canary Islands. If the men were
the same person, this would be the first documented pre-Puerto Rico origin in
the direct line.

## Why it is not attached to the family

The burial calls Joseph Roque the widower of María Herrera. The direct
ancestor is paired with Marciana Delgado on Luís’s death record. Neither
record supplies an age, parents, occupation, residence, child, or other bridge
that could show a second marriage or otherwise unite the identities.

The combination of a common given name and a highly attractive origin clue is
not enough. The record is retained as a same-name candidate and rejected as
the target Roque unless a later source connects Joseph to Marciana or Luís.

The Canary Islands remain a tested but unproven route.
