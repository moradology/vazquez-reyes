# Family Echo exports: leads for extending both lines

Date: 24 July 2026

## Scope and privacy

The supplied Vázquez and Reyes TXT/CSV exports were created in Family Echo.
They contain family-compiled relationships without source citations, along
with information about living people. The raw exports remain outside the
public repository. Only deceased historical leads needed for the research
questions are summarized here.

No person or relationship from these exports is promoted into the canonical
people or relationships files without independent record support.

## Vázquez branch

The export agrees with the documented relationship of Rafael Vázquez to Juan
Vázquez and Carlina. It supplies a reported full-sibling cluster:

- Inez, later Rosario;
- Juana, later Rosario;
- Josué Vázquez;
- Marcelo Vázquez;
- “Paulo” Vázquez, marked female and therefore possibly Paula or another
  transcription error;
- Rafael Vázquez;
- Ramón Vázquez.

The export gives Juan's parents as Carmen Rodríguez and “Soltero Vázquez.”
Carmen Rodríguez is consistent with the documentary form **Juan Vázquez y
Rodríguez**, but that consistency is not independent proof. “Soltero” is not
safe to treat as a given name: in Spanish it is ordinarily a marital-status
descriptor, and the tree contains other placeholders and duplicate Juans.

The export also assigns multiple partners and another son named Juan to Juan
Vázquez. Those entries may represent distinct men or conflated generations and
must remain separate candidates.

## Reyes branch

The export agrees that Cruz was a daughter of Mauricio Reyes and Carmen Díaz.
It supplies broad, unsourced life years:

- Mauricio Reyes: about 1890–1982;
- Carmen Díaz Reyes: about 1895–1955.

It reports these full siblings of Cruz:

- Ruperto Reyes, born 1912;
- Belén Reyes, later Colón, born 1917;
- Angelina Reyes, later Álvarez, born 1918;
- Cándido Reyes, born 1925;
- Erebeda Reyes;
- “Jauna” Reyes, likely a spelling error;
- Julio Reyes.

The dated siblings give useful anchors for census and civil-registration
searches. Most importantly, a sister named **Belén Reyes** offers a concrete
alternative explanation for the original Belén clue: the remembered name may
refer to Cruz's sister rather than Carmen Díaz's maiden name. This remains a
hypothesis until Belén's and Cruz's original records name the same parents.

The export does not identify parents for Mauricio or Carmen. It also does not
explain the separate family report that Mauricio's mother had the surname
Martínez.

## Image preservation

Every relevant census sheet, civil registration, marriage record, church
record, and candidate image will be retained locally, even when rejected or
unused. The ignored image manifest records capture status and disposition.
Full subscription captures remain private; any image selected for the public
family page requires a separate rights and privacy review.
