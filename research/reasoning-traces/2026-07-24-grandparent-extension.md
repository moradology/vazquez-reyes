# Grandparent extension and sibling clusters

Research date: 24 July 2026

## Scope

Extend the four parental lines behind Mauricio Reyes Martínez, Carmen Díaz
García, Juan Vázquez Rodríguez, and Carlina Perales Pérez. Record birth and
death information explicitly, including unknown fields, and collect siblings
as a secondary line of inquiry.

## Pedro Reyes and Ana Martínez

Pedro's 3 January 1918 death act and Ana's 21 March 1918 death act describe the
same Humacao couple. Ana's act names children Isabel, Natalio, Mauricio, and
Juliano; Natalio's 1904 marriage independently names Pedro and Ana.

Conclusion: the couple and Mauricio relationship are documented. Birth years
are estimates from reported ages. Neither death act identifies Ana's parents;
Pedro's parents were unknown to his informant.

## Lope Díaz Figueroa and Reyes García Olivero

The 1907 Mauricio–Carmen marriage and 1910 household establish Lope and Reyes
as Carmen's parents. Separate death acts provide their death dates and extend
the lines to Manuel Díaz and Carmen Figueroa, and Bautista García and Carmen
Olivero. A death record for Lope's brother Pedro repeats Manuel and Carmen and
adds Naguabo origins.

Conclusion: the direct links are strong. Carmen, Saturnino, and Paulino are
known children, not a complete sibling set.

## Sotero Vázquez and the Rodríguez conflict

Both Juan's 1902 marriage and 1951 death name Sotero as his father, but the
mother changes from Carmen Rodríguez to María Eugenia Rodríguez. Braulio
Vázquez Rodríguez's original 1915 and 1923 marriages name Sotero and María
Eugenia as his parents.

Conclusion: María Eugenia is documented as Braulio's mother. That fact does
not prove she was Juan's mother; Juan and Braulio could have been half
brothers, or one of Juan's records could be wrong. No mother is merged into
Juan's direct line until his Yabucoa baptism or birth record is found.

## Marcelino Perales y Medina and Aurora Pérez

Marcelino's 13 May 1891 death act spans two filmed pages. The first page gives
his identity, Naguabo origin, age, and parents; the continuation names his
widow Aurora Pérez and children. Independent civil records repeatedly name
Marcelino or Marcelo Perales and Aurora Pérez as parents of Carlina and a wider
child cluster.

Conclusion: the death record is accepted as Carlina's father. His father's
given name remains transcribed as Abal or Abel pending a clearer comparison.
Aurora's own life dates are still unknown.
