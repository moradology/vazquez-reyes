# Bayamón 1781 marriage candidate and Luciano collateral

## Question

Is the Francisco Vázquez–María Magdalena Cortés couple who married in Bayamón
on 2 January 1781 the same couple documented in Caguas from 1786 through 1801?

## Direct-line baseline

The direct line remains anchored by original Caguas and Humacao records:

- Miguel's 1786 Caguas baptism names Francisco Vázquez and María Magdalena.
- María de los Ángeles's 1793 Caguas burial repeats the couple.
- María Magdalena Cortés's 1794 Caguas burial calls her Francisco Vázquez's
  legitimate wife.
- José Vázquez's 1801 Caguas burial names Francisco and María Cortés.
- Máximo's 1805 Humacao marriage names his parents as Francisco and María
  Cortez, and an 1819 child baptism calls Máximo a native of Caguas.

Those records establish the Caguas household. They do not state either
parent's birthplace, parents, or marriage.

## The Bayamón marriage

A focused FamilySearch public-discovery search returned persistent record
`X9P2-28TG`:

- marriage: 2 January 1781;
- place: Bayamón, Puerto Rico;
- groom: Francisco Vázquez Hernández;
- bride: María Magdalena Cortez;
- groom's parents as indexed: Juan Vázquez del Río and María Hernández; and
- bride's parents as indexed: Lázaro Cortez and Isabel Monzor.

The discovery result flattens both parent pairs into its father and mother
fields, so the manuscript remains the authority for the family structure.

The same event appears in an Ancestry member tree with a cropped parish-register
image. The image visibly carries the year 1781, the Francisco and María
Magdalena Cortés pair, and both parent pairs. It reads the bride's mother more
like Isabel Monzón than the index's `Monzor`. The copy is 1486 by 1388 pixels,
has yellow highlighting and a visible pointer, and is not a complete fresh
capture of the register page.

The public Bayamón register inventory says the 1770–1787 marriage book begins
at FamilySearch film-browser image 1080. The exact frame for this event has not
yet been recovered because the original viewer redirects to sign-in.

## Identity analysis

The candidate has real weight:

1. The complete couple name is unusually specific.
2. The 1781 marriage precedes the first known Caguas child in 1786.
3. Bayamón-to-Caguas movement is geographically possible.
4. The candidate comes from an indexed parish event plus an image of the
   manuscript, not from a bare member-tree assertion.

It is not yet a direct-line event:

1. No reviewed record calls either spouse a native or resident of Caguas.
2. No child, witness, godparent, sibling, or later record joins the Bayamón
   couple to the Caguas household.
3. The exact full FamilySearch frame and adjacent entries remain unread.
4. A same-name couple is still possible, especially across separate parishes.

The marriage is therefore a **strong unmerged same-couple candidate**. Juan
Vázquez del Río, María Hernández, Lázaro Cortés, and Isabel Monzón are not
promoted as Máximo's grandparents.

## Luciano Vázquez collateral

FamilySearch public discovery also returned `XSQC-2ZML` for Luciano Vázquez:

- buried 17 June 1839 in Gurabo;
- reported age 65;
- native of Bayamón; and
- associated in the derivative index with Juan José Vázquez, Marcela
  Hernández, and María de la Cruz Torres.

That derivative relationship order is wrong. The low-resolution manuscript
copy reads Luciano as the legitimate son of **Juan José** and **María de la
Cruz Hernández**, married to **Marcela de Torres**. This aligns the Hernández
mother with the 1781 marriage more closely and suggests a possible Francisco
sibling network. It does not prove that Juan José was Juan Vázquez del Río, or
that Luciano's Francisco was the direct Caguas man.

## Origin boundary

Even if the candidate is eventually proved, it moves the working line from
Caguas to Bayamón and possibly Toa Alta context within Puerto Rico. It does not
identify an ancestor born in Europe, Africa, the Canary Islands, or another
Caribbean colony.
