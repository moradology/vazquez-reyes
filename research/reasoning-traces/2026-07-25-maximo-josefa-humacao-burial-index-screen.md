# Máximo Vázquez and Josefa Rivera: Humacao burial-index screen

Date: 2026-07-25

## Question

Do the surviving alphabetical burial indexes for Dulce Nombre de Jesús in Humacao identify a burial for Máximo Vázquez or Josefa Rivera after the couple's last presently documented child?

## Material reviewed

The already captured, full index-book runs were screened specifically for the target names and their common spelling forms:

- 1815–1820
- 1823–1826
- 1826–1829
- 1829–1836
- 1836–1839
- 1839–1843
- 1843–1846
- 1846–1852

For Máximo, the Vázquez, Vasquez, and Basquez sections were checked for Máximo, Maximino, Maximiliano, and close handwritten forms. For Josefa, the Rivera and Ribera sections were checked for Josefa and María Josefa.

Each directory has a SHA-256 manifest. The separately retained 1852–1857 partial capture reaches only the earlier alphabetic sections and does not cover Rivera or Vázquez.

## Result

No defensible entry for either Máximo Vázquez or Josefa Rivera was found in the eight complete index books.

This is a bounded negative search result, not evidence that either person was absent from Humacao or alive after 1852.

## Limits

- The retained run contains no 1821–1822 index book.
- An alphabetical index can omit an entry that exists in the body of a register.
- Some handwriting is faint, crowded, or damaged.
- A burial may survive only in another copy, another parish, a neighboring municipality, or a civil or diocesan record.
- A surname or given name could have been written in an unexpected form.

## Consequence for the next search

Do not repeatedly search the same Humacao index run as though it were unreviewed. Give priority to:

1. unindexed or duplicate Humacao material, especially the 1821–1822 gap;
2. Yabucoa, Juncos, Las Piedras, and San Lorenzo registers;
3. records of the couple's later children that might state whether a parent was deceased or where the parents lived;
4. Máximo's missing baptism or a duplicate/diocesan copy, because it remains the strongest route to his parents' native places and the earlier Vázquez line.
