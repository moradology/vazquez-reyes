# Sotero Vázquez death and the Rodríguez family

Research date: 24 July 2026

## Question

Do the records support Carmen Rodríguez and María Eugenia Rodríguez as two
different mothers, or as names used for the same woman?

## Sotero Vázquez Rodríguez

The original Humacao death act identifies Sotero Vázquez Rodríguez as a
Yabucoa native, age 56, son of Atilano Vázquez and Juana Rodríguez. He died
31 May 1916 of a cerebral hemorrhage. The act names his wife as Carmen
Rodríguez and names seven living children: Juan, Juana, Victorio, Carmelo,
Mauricio, Braulio, and Francisco.

The reported age implies birth around 1860. That estimate is not promoted to an
exact birth year because it sits awkwardly beside Sotero's 1875 marriage. No
defensible baptism has yet been found.

## Sotero Vázquez and María Eugenia Rodríguez

Their Yabucoa marriage entry falls between 16 and 25 September 1875. It names
Sotero's parents as Atilano Vázquez and Juana Rodríguez. María Eugenia's parents
are Cristóbal Rodríguez and Leonor Díaz.

## Juan de la Rosa Vázquez

Juan was born 30 August 1878 and baptized 13 October 1878. The register names
Sotero Vázquez and Carmen Rodríguez as his parents, and Cristóbal Rodríguez and
María Pacheco as his maternal grandparents.

## Victorio Vázquez Rodríguez

Victorio was born 22 December 1880 and baptized 27 February 1881. The register
names Sotero Vázquez and María Eugenia Rodríguez as his parents, and Cristóbal
Rodríguez and Leonarda Díaz as his maternal grandparents.

## Juan del Carmen Vázquez y Rodríguez

Juan del Carmen was born 24 May 1885. The civil act names Sotero Vázquez and
Eugenia Rodríguez as his parents, and Cristóbal Rodríguez and Leonarda Pacheco
as his maternal grandparents. A FamilySearch tree attachment calls him Carmelo,
and Sotero's death names both the older Juan and a Carmelo. That adult identity
is plausible, but the derivative attachment does not prove it.

## Francisco Vázquez Rodríguez

Francisco was born 3 June 1892. His civil act names Sotero Vázquez and Eugenia
Rodríguez as his parents, and Cristóbal Rodríguez and María Leonarda Díaz as
his maternal grandparents. The act says all four grandparents were deceased by
23 June 1892.

## Working conclusion

The same husband, recurring father Cristóbal Rodríguez, complementary
Díaz/Pacheco forms for the maternal grandmother, chronological continuity, and
matching child cluster strongly favor one woman recorded as Carmen, Eugenia,
and María Eugenia. The likely maternal name may itself involve Díaz and
Pacheco.

This is a strong same-person hypothesis, not a completed merge. No reviewed
record explicitly says Carmen María Eugenia Rodríguez or María Leonarda Díaz
Pacheco. The separate profiles preserve that evidentiary gap.
