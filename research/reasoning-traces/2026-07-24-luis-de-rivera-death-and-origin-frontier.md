# Luís de Rivera's 1811 death and the origin frontier

## Question

Can the Luís de Rivera and Isidora Rodríguez line be carried one generation
farther, and does any record identify a direct ancestor from outside Puerto
Rico?

## Original record

FamilySearch full-text search surfaced a death entry in the Humacao register
for 1811:

- DGS 008038524, item 6, image 23
- stable image:
  https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSFS-2BP2?view=fullText&keywords=Isidora+Rodr%C3%ADguez%2Cde+Lu%C3%ADs+de+Rivera&lang=en&groupId=M993-WQQ

The original entry begins near the bottom of the left page and continues at
the top of the right page. The key clauses identify:

- **Luís de Rivera**, native of "este Partido," meaning the Humacao district;
- about **50 years old**, supporting only an approximate birth around 1761;
- legitimate son of **Roque** and **Marciana Delgado**;
- church-married to **Isidora Rodríguez**; and
- nine children: **Josefa, Simona, Ysabel, Juana, Gerónima, Domingo, María,
  Ramón, and Juan**.

The manuscript reads Marciana. The automated transcript's "Merenciana" form is
not adopted. The exact day is too faint to promote; 1811 is secure from the
register and surrounding entry.

## Identity

The record is a strong match to the established household:

1. The decedent is written as Luís de Rivera, the full form already documented
   in the 1790 and 1792 Las Piedras baptisms.
2. His wife is Isidora Rodríguez.
3. The children include Josefa, Simona, and Ysabel, each independently tied to
   that parent pair by earlier originals.
4. The additional six children are accepted from the death entry but are
   retained without surnames until their own records are found.

## Negative coverage

The Las Piedras baptism review was completed through all 227 images in the
surviving 1778–1799 item. It found Simona and Ysabel but not Josefa. This is a
complete review of one item, not evidence that Josefa lacked a baptism or was
born elsewhere.

Focused FamilySearch index and OCR searches tested:

- Isidora and Ysidora Rodríguez in early Humacao deaths;
- Marciana Delgado with Roque;
- María Cortez and Cortés in early Caguas deaths;
- Máximo with Francisco and María Cortez; and
- Josefa with Luís and Isidora Rodríguez.

An 1827 Humacao death for an Isidora Rodríguez names husband Andrés Obierna
and different parents and is rejected. No indexed target record survived the
name, spouse, parent, date, and place checks. These searches are negative
coverage only because indexing, OCR, and surviving-book gaps are substantial.

## Conclusion

The direct line advances one generation from Luís to **Roque** and **Marciana
Delgado**. It does not cross Puerto Rico's boundary. Luís is explicitly native
to the Humacao district, while the birthplaces of Roque, Marciana, Isidora,
Francisco, and María Cortez remain unknown.

No named direct ancestor is yet documented as born in Africa, Europe, the
Canary Islands, another Caribbean island, or anywhere else outside Puerto
Rico. Those remain search routes, not family conclusions.
