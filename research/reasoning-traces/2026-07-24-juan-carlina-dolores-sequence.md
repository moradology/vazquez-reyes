# Juan Vázquez: Carlina first, Dolores later

- Juan Vázquez Rodríguez and Carlina Perales Pérez married civilly in Humacao
  on 25 September 1902.
- Carlina died in Humacao on 31 May 1922.
- The 1930 Mambiche census places Juan with Dolores Rivera and children using
  both Vázquez Perales and Vázquez Rivera. Both adults are marked `CC`,
  *contrato consensual*.
- The 1940 Antón Ruíz census again places Juan and Dolores together and again
  marks both `CC`; FamilySearch renders this as “common law.”
- The supplied family tree independently remembered Dolores or Lola as Juan’s
  partner.

## Presentation decision

Show two distinct households in order. Describe Carlina as Juan’s documented
wife and Dolores as his later documented partner or common-law household.
Do not claim a second legal marriage unless a marriage act is found.
