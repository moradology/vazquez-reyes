# Reyes-Díaz household in 1935 and 1940

Research date: 24 July 2026

This trace keeps each parental pair and each child identification separate. It
does not merge similar names merely because the ages are compatible.

## Mauricio Reyes Martínez and Carmen Díaz García

The 1935 Puerto Rico social census places Mauricio Reyes, age 46, and Carmen
Díaz, age 40, in Collores, Humacao. Children in the household are Cruz,
Eriberta or Heriberta, Belén, Cándido, Saturnino, and Julio César. A grandson
and a lodger also appear.

The 4 April 1940 census places the same parental pair in Collores. The original
image, which controls over the imperfect automated extraction, gives these
children on rows 93-97:

- Eriberta Reyes Díaz, age 23
- Cruz Reyes Díaz, age 21
- Aurora Reyes Díaz, age 19
- Cándido Reyes Díaz, age 16
- Julio C. Reyes Díaz, age 9

Three grandchildren follow on rows 98-100. Their names and exact details are
not needed for the direct-line reconstruction.

The two censuses extend the known household beyond 1930 and place Pastora,
civilly recorded as Cruz, in Puerto Rico on 4 April 1940. Her Manhattan
marriage-license affidavit is dated 21 October 1941 and her marriage occurred
25 October. The evidence therefore bounds her move from Puerto Rico to New York
between those dates; it does not identify a ship, flight, or exact travel date.

## Cándido Reyes Díaz

Humacao birth act 97 was registered 10 September 1923 from a declaration by
Mauricio Reyes Martínez. It states that Cándido was born 14 July 1923 at 10
p.m. and names his mother as Carmen Díaz García. It also repeats both sets of
grandparents:

- Mauricio's parents: Pedro Reyes and Ana Martínez
- Carmen's parents: Lope Díaz and Reyes García

The index mistranscribes the male child's name as Cándida. The original image
controls.

A 19 November 1948 Humacao death certificate calls him Cándido Reyes López,
reports a 12 June 1923 birth, names father Mauricio Reyes and mother Carmen
López Díaz, and uses Saturnino Reyes as informant. The surname and birth date
conflict with the original birth act. The following independent details support
the same-person conclusion:

- given name Cándido
- age 25 in 1948
- Humacao residence and birthplace
- father Mauricio Reyes
- mother Carmen
- brother Saturnino as informant
- continuous placement in Mauricio and Carmen's 1930, 1935, and 1940 household

The death is therefore attached with explicit maternal-surname and birth-date
conflicts. A sensitive identifier visible on the death certificate is neither
transcribed nor published.

## Teresa Reyes Díaz

Teresa's original Humacao death act says she died 1 June 1927 at one day old
and was the legitimate daughter of Mauricio Reyes and Carmen Díaz. It confirms
a previously unknown infant daughter.

Age one day supports only an approximate birth range of 31 May to 1 June 1927.
An exact date should not be assigned without a birth or baptism record.

## Remaining name questions among the children

The household evidence now supports a broad sibling cluster, but two pairs
remain unresolved:

- Angelina, age about eight in 1930, may be Aurora, age 19 in 1940.
- Tomás, an infant in 1930, may be Julio César, age five in 1935 and nine in
  1940.

The age patterns are compatible, but compatibility is not identity. Each pair
remains separate until a birth, baptism, death, marriage, or other direct record
connects the names.

The Saturnino recorded as Mauricio and Carmen's son in 1930 and 1935 is not
merged with Saturnino Reyes Romero, the grandson recorded in 1940.

## Mauricio's 1977 death candidate

The 29 May 1977 Bayamón death certificate for Mauricio Reyes Martínez is now
the strongest death candidate rather than a weak rejected lead. It agrees on:

- exact full name
- a birth about 1881, compatible with the 1907 marriage age and 1923 birth-act
  age
- Humacao birthplace
- father Pedro Reyes
- widowed status

It calls his mother María Martínez rather than Ana Martínez and does not name
his spouse. The informant was a nurse, not a relative, and explicitly did not
know the spouse. That makes the parental conflict weaker than the closer 1907
marriage and 1923 child-birth records, both of which name Ana.

The candidate is not fully merged because the family export reports an
unsourced 1982 death year and no spouse or child appears on the 1977
certificate. A baptism, obituary, burial record, or family-informed later record
could settle the remaining conflict.

## Image handling

Full record pages, viewer captures, and original-resolution downloads for all
five records remain in the private evidence archive. The public derivative of
the 1940 census is an unaltered crop of rows 91-96 only. It ends with Cándido
and excludes Julio and the grandchildren because their deaths have not been
documented.
