# Cruz Reyes baptism index and damaged folio

Research date: 24 July 2026

## Question

Did the Gurabo baptismal record use Pastora, Cruz, or a compound form for the
girl born 3 May 1915 to Mauricio Reyes Martínez and Carmen Díaz García?

## Index search

The alphabetical baptism index for San José, Gurabo, was reviewed on
FamilySearch:

- DGS 008126783, film 1389060, item 8
- `San Jose. Baptism Record Indexes 1881–1928`
- index images 117 and 118

The simple Reyes entries span the bottom of image 117 and continue before the
compound Reyes surnames on image 118. They include two entries named Cruz:

- `REYES | Cruz | 13 | 7 v`
- `REYES | Cruz | 15 | 18 v`

No Pastora Reyes entry appears in the reviewed Reyes section. The book 15
entry is the only Cruz Reyes entry in the later volume and is the plausible
candidate for the child born in 1915. The index alone does not give parents,
birth date, or enough detail to identify the child conclusively.

## Register search

The catalog shows that DGS 008126783 contains six baptism items covering
1914–1938. Book 15, folio 18 verso maps to:

- item 2, `Gurabo. Baptism Records 1916–1922`
- local item image 13
- film frame 00125
- FamilySearch ARK `3:1:3Q9M-CSVK-1SXR-H`

The manuscript is visibly marked `18 v` on the left page. The page survives in
poor condition: sheets are torn, folded, taped, and overlapped. The visible
entries and automated transcript do not expose a legible Cruz Reyes entry or
the names Mauricio Reyes and Carmen Díaz. Adjacent item image 12 was also
captured because it shows another arrangement of the damaged folio, but it
does not recover the missing entry.

## Conclusion

This search changes the name question, but does not close it:

- A matching-era Gurabo baptism index points to a child recorded as **Cruz
  Reyes**, book 15, folio 18 verso.
- The exact folio exists and was captured.
- The individual entry is not legible in the available scan, so the index
  candidate cannot yet be tied conclusively to Mauricio and Carmen's daughter.
- No Pastora Reyes entry was found in the reviewed Reyes index section.

The index supports Cruz as the likely church-indexed name. Pastora remains only
a secondhand recollection from Cruz's daughter, who does not remember Cruz
using it; no reviewed record supports it. A duplicate parish register,
sacramental certificate, alternate filming, or parish archive lookup is needed
to identify the child and recover the complete baptismal wording without
assuming what name it will contain.
