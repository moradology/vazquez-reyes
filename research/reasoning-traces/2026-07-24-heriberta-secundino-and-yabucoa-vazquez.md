# Heriberta, Secundino, and the Yabucoa Vázquez line

Date: 24 July 2026

## Purpose

Move beyond census-only sibling lists and test the next Vázquez generation with original parish and civil records.

## Heriberta Reyes Díaz

Two original records identify the same daughter of Mauricio Reyes Martínez and Carmen Díaz:

- Naguabo civil act 593 was registered 2 September 1913. Mauricio declared that Heriberta was born 25 July 1913 at 10 p.m. The act names grandparents Pedro Reyes and Ana Martínez, and Lope Díaz and Reyes García.
- Naguabo baptism entry 713 records the baptism on 31 August 1913 but gives the birth as 16 March 1913. It names the same parents and grandparents, using Anastasia Martínez and María de Reyes García.

The exact name, parents, and all four grandparents make the identity secure. The birth date is not secure. The civil and parish dates remain side by side until another contemporary record resolves them.

## Secundino Díaz García

A 1903 Humacao baptism records Secundino, born 23 May and baptized 4 July 1903, as a legitimate son of Lope Díaz and María de Reyes García. The computer index reads Decundino, but the handwritten margin reads Secundino.

The entry expands Lope's father from Manuel Díaz to Manuel María Díaz and names the grandparents as Manuel María Díaz and Carmen Figueroa, and Bautista García and Dolores Olivero. Reyes García's 1933 death act instead names her mother Carmen Olivero. Dolores and Carmen are retained as an unresolved maternal-given-name conflict.

## Juan de la Rosa Vázquez

Yabucoa baptism entry 523 records Juan de la Rosa Vázquez:

- born 30 August 1878;
- baptized 13 October 1878;
- legitimate son of Sotero Vázquez and Carmen Rodríguez;
- paternal grandparents Atilano Vázquez and Juana Rodríguez;
- maternal grandparents Cristóbal Rodríguez and María Pacheco.

This is the direct record requested by the open case. It confirms Juan's date, place, father, and the Carmen form used in his 1902 marriage. It does not resolve the 1951 death record's María Eugenia form.

## Sotero Vázquez and María Eugenia Rodríguez

Yabucoa marriage entry 440 records Sotero Vázquez, son of Atilano Vázquez and Juana Rodríguez, marrying María Eugenia Rodríguez, daughter of Cristóbal Rodríguez and Leonor Díaz.

The scribe omitted the entry's date. The preceding dated entry is 16 September 1875; entry 442 on the next image is dated 25 September 1875. The marriage is therefore recorded only as occurring within that interval.

The marriage and Juan's baptism independently confirm Atilano Vázquez and Juana Rodríguez as Sotero's parents. They conflict on the Rodríguez woman:

- Juan's baptism: Carmen Rodríguez, daughter of Cristóbal Rodríguez and María Pacheco.
- Sotero's marriage: María Eugenia Rodríguez, daughter of Cristóbal Rodríguez and Leonor Díaz.
- Juan's 1951 death: María Eugenia Rodríguez.
- The 1910 census uses María Rodríguez y Díaz.
- María Eugenia's reviewed 1933 death act uses the full form María Eugenia
  Rodríguez Pacheco, calls her Sotero Vázquez's widow, names María Pacheco as
  her mother, and was reported by son Francisco.

The widow and son-as-informant details strongly favor one woman under alternate
given names. No reviewed record explicitly combines Carmen with Eugenia or
explains the Díaz and Pacheco forms, so the project does not merge the profiles
or their mothers.
