# Ramón Reyes and Inés Castro death-index sweep

Research date: 25 July 2026

## Question

Can the Puerto Rico civil-registration death index identify the Ramón Reyes
and Inés Castro named as Pedro Reyes's parents in two original Humacao
baptisms?

## Known anchor

Mauricio Reyes Martínez's 1882 baptism and Benito Isabel Reyes Martínez's
1877 baptism independently name their paternal grandparents as Ramón Reyes
and Inés Castro. Both entries identify Pedro Reyes as their son. Those
grandparent statements remain the direct evidence for the target couple.

## Method

The Ancestry cockpit searched collection 9100 one year at a time with exact
name, exact death year, and exact Humacao death place:

- Ramón Reyes: 1885 through 1910;
- Inés Castro: 1885 through 1910;
- Pascasio Reyes: 1885 through 1889, after that name appeared in the Inés
  candidate's original.

The year-by-year method was necessary because an unbounded first results page
is nationally sorted and incomplete, while an exact year-and-place search
returns a bounded result set for that indexed combination.

## Inés Castro Rivera candidate

The Inés screen produced one record: Humacao act 385 for Inés Castro Rivera,
reported age 85 and a native of Las Piedras. The act was opened and both filmed
pages were downloaded at original size. It is dated 13 August 1889 and says
that she died at nine in the morning on the previous day; the derivative index
uses 13 August as the death date.

This woman fits the broad place and generation, but the original calls her the
widow of **Pascasio Reyes**. The two direct descendant baptisms instead name
the target husband as **Ramón Reyes**. No reviewed record combines Pascasio
with Ramón, identifies Pascasio as an alternate given name, or joins this
woman to Pedro Reyes. The candidate is therefore preserved but not merged.

The index associates Florencio and Baltazara with the record, but those
relationships are not used to extend the direct tree while the woman's own
identity remains unresolved.

## Ramón Reyes result

The Ramón screen produced only one exact-name Humacao death in the entire
1885–1910 interval: Ramón Reyes y Ruiz, who died 6 May 1898 at age 18 and was
born about 1880. He is far too young to be the father of Pedro Reyes, who was
already an adult with a child by about 1867, and is rejected as the target.

## Pascasio Reyes result

No exact Pascasio Reyes death was indexed in Humacao from 1885 through 1889.
That bounded negative does not show when or where the husband named in act 385
died. He may have died before civil registration began, outside Humacao, under
a variant name, or in an omitted record.

## Conclusion

The civil index does not identify either target life event. Inés Castro Rivera
is the only generation-fitting exact-name death found, but the husband conflict
is material and prevents a merge. The target Ramón Reyes and Inés Castro still
require parish-era records.

## Next record

Prioritize Pedro Reyes's baptism and his marriage to Juana Villafañe or Ana
Martínez, then search pre-1885 Humacao and Las Piedras marriage and burial
registers for Ramón Reyes with Inés Castro and for Pascasio Reyes with Inés
Castro Rivera. A record explicitly joining Ramón and Pascasio, or naming Pedro
as a child of the 1889 woman, is required before the candidate can be attached.
