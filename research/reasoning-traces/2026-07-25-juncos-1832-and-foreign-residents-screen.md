# Juncos 1832 census and Puerto Rico foreign-resident screen

Date: 25 July 2026

## Question

Do the complete published transcriptions of the 1832 Juncos census or Puerto
Rico's 1815–1845 foreign-resident files identify Máximo Vázquez, Josefa Rivera,
their children, or an earlier overseas origin?

## Material preserved

Two complete volunteer finding aids were downloaded and hash-pinned:

- *Juncos – Censo 1832 (Film #1511631)*, 50 pages;
- *Compendio: Puerto Rico, Registro de Extranjeros, 1815–1845*, 20 pages.

Text was extracted from every page. Relevant transcription pages were also
rendered as PNG files so the table layout and image references are preserved.
These are finding aids, not substitutes for the handwritten originals.

## Juncos census result

The transcription contains no named Máximo Vázquez, Josefa Rivera, or Atilano
Vázquez household that can be assigned to the direct family.

The superficially relevant entries were separated explicitly:

- the only named Atilano is **Atilano Piñero**, age 40, in barrio Ceiba;
- a **Francisco Vázquez**, age 35, appears as a household head in barrio
  Gurabo on referenced image 2030, followed by a partly unreadable woman and
  two-year-old Andrés Avelino;
- the same page contains several other Vázquez household heads and a
  **Magdalena Delgado**, age 70.

None supplies the required parent, spouse, child, or prior-record bridge.
Francisco is not Francisco Solano Vázquez Rivera, who was reported age 40 at
death in 1878, and the age is also a poor fit for Máximo's father Francisco.
Magdalena and Marciana are distinct given names and are not merged.

The negative result is bounded. The transcription omits repeated surnames for
many spouses and children, contains unreadable fields, and may contain copying
errors. A person not named as a household head can therefore remain
unidentifiable in the table. The result means only that no defensible household
assignment can be made from this transcription.

## Foreign-resident result

The complete compendium was screened for the direct early names and surname
variants: Vázquez, Vasquez, Bázquez, Basquez, Rivera, Ribera, Cortés, Cortez,
Delgado, Rodríguez, Máximo, Josefa, Francisco, María, Luís, Isidora, Roque,
Marciana, Atilano, and Juana.

It contains no exact direct-line ancestor. The only Vázquez-like applicant is
**Juan Luis Bázquez**, filed in 1824 at collection image 423. No reviewed family
record names Juan Luis or otherwise connects that applicant to the Caguas,
Humacao, Juncos, San Lorenzo, or Yabucoa households. He remains detached.

This collection is not a census of every overseas-born resident. It is a
surviving set of foreign-resident files, so a negative name screen cannot prove
that every earlier ancestor was Puerto Rico-born.

## Result

- No pre-Puerto Rico birthplace or immigrant ancestor was established.
- No 1832 household was attached to the family.
- Three tempting census near-matches and one foreign-resident surname match
  were documented and kept separate.
- The strongest next actions remain original-image work: María Josefa
  Vázquez's 1826 San Lorenzo baptism and the exact Juncos and Yabucoa burial
  ranges for Máximo and Josefa.
