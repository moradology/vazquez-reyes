# Initial identification of Cruz Reyes and Rafael Vázquez

Date: 2026-07-23

## Question

Can the sparse family account be connected to one documented couple without
silently resolving the conflicting names and dates?

## Identification

The match is strong. The 25 October 1941 Manhattan marriage license names
Rafael Vazquez and Cruz Reyes as spouses. It gives Rafael's parents as Juan
Vazquez and Carlina Vazquez and Cruz's parents as Mauricio Reyes and Carmen
Reyes. Cruz's Social Security claim independently gives Mauricio Reyes and
Carmen Diaz. The 1950 census then records Raphael and Cruz Vasquez as a married
Manhattan household. Finally, the cemetery's official lookup puts Cruz and
Rafael in different positions within the same `WIN4T-22-11` plot.

This combination is substantially stronger than a name-and-date match:

1. Both supplied father names appear in the couple's marriage record.
2. Cruz's maiden surname and both of her parents are independently repeated.
3. Rafael's Puerto Rico census household supplies the fuller Hispanic surnames
   for both parents.
4. The couple appears together after marriage.
5. Their same-plot burials are separated by dates consistent with the supplied
   death years.

## Name resolutions

- Cruz's mother is provisionally **Carmen Díaz**, also recorded as Carmen
  Reyes in married-name form. The Belén clue is not discarded, but no reviewed
  record yet explains it.
- Rafael's mother is strongly identified as **Carlina Perales y Pérez**. The
  family memory “Cathelina/Catalina, Pérez or López” likely preserved Pérez and
  an approximate first name. No reviewed source supports López.
- Rafael's father is repeatedly **Juan Vázquez y Rodríguez**. “Juan Ramón
  Vázquez” remains a family-supplied variant until an original vital record
  confirms or rejects the middle name.

## Date conflicts

### Cruz

- Family account/public SSDI derivative: 3 May 1915.
- Social Security claim: 4 May 1914.
- 1941 marriage license: May 1917.

The Social Security claim is provisionally preferred because it also identifies
both parents and birthplace. None of these derivatives replaces an original
Humacao birth registration.

### Rafael

- Puerto Rico civil-registration index: February 1906.
- 1910 and 1920 census ages: consistent with 1906.
- Family account: 24 October 1906.
- 1941 marriage license: 24 October 1909.
- Public SSDI derivative: 28 February 1906.
- One subscription SSDI transcription: 29 February 1906, an impossible date
  because 1906 was not a leap year.

The safest current statement is **born February 1906 in Humacao, exact day
unresolved**. The recurrence of 24 October in family memory and the marriage
record may explain the remembered date, but it is inconsistent with the civil
registration month.

Rafael's family-supplied death date, 30 December 1984, fits the official burial
on 2 January 1985. A public SSDI derivative instead says 1 December, so the
exact death date remains probable rather than proved.

## Geographic narrative

Rafael was enumerated as a child in Anton Ruiz, Humacao, in 1910 and 1920, and
as a single adult in Mambiche, Humacao, in 1930. He and Cruz married in
Manhattan in 1941 and lived on East 109th Street by 1950. Their later Social
Security residence is indexed as ZIP 10009. They were buried in Linden, New
Jersey, but no evidence reviewed so far demonstrates that they lived in New
Jersey.

## Negative and limited searches

- No confident entries were found in the reviewed New Jersey death-index image
  sets for 1984 and 1998. This does not disprove New Jersey deaths, but the New
  York evidence and cemetery transport dates make New York deaths more likely.
- Public Puerto Rico archive searching did not immediately locate Cruz's birth
  under the tried variants. Spanish two-surname forms, V/S variants, and
  indexing gaps require a more targeted Humacao search.
- The cemetery database is stronger than the user-contributed memorial for
  burial date and division. The family description “Rosehill” is
  understandable because the combined property is named Rosedale & Rosehill;
  the official plot entries are under Rosedale.
