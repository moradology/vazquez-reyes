# Aurora Pérez death-index sweep, 1940–1950

## Question

When and where did Aurora Pérez, widow of Marcelino Perales, die?

## Firm anchors

- Aurora was alive on 5 April 1940 in Río Blanco, Naguabo, in the household of her daughter Hilaria or Ilaria Perales.
- The 1940 census identifies her as Aurora Pérez, widow of Perales.
- The 1935 and 1940 censuses report ages 95 and 99. Those figures suggest a birth about 1840–1841, but they are late-life estimates rather than exact birth evidence.
- Marcelino Perales died in Naguabo in 1891.

## Bounded Ancestry index screen

The Puerto Rico civil-registration index (collection 9100) was searched one death year at a time.

### Exact Naguabo death place, 1940–1950

An exact given-name search for Aurora, with exact death year and exact Naguabo death place, produced:

- 1940: no indexed result
- 1941: no indexed result
- 1942: Aurora Monserrate Pérez, record 4622921
- 1943: no indexed result
- 1944: no indexed result
- 1945: Aurora Rivera Otero, record 4625083
- 1946: no indexed result
- 1947: no indexed result
- 1948: Aurora Telmont Romas, record 4625719
- 1949: no indexed result
- 1950: no indexed result

All three returned records are different women. The 1942 record names the deceased woman's spouse as Marcelo Pérez, not Marcelino Perales. The 1945 and 1948 records have different surnames and identities.

Exact searches for Aurora Pérez and Aurora Perales in Naguabo were also run for 1940–1945. They produced no additional candidate.

### Exact Aurora Pérez elsewhere in Puerto Rico, 1940–1945

An exact full-name search across Puerto Rico, one exact death year at a time, returned unrelated women in Gurabo, Camuy, Santurce, Quebradillas, Ponce, and Ceiba, plus the already rejected 1942 Naguabo record. None carried a spouse, child, residence, age, or other identity bridge to the direct Aurora.

### Age-weighted candidates reviewed in the originals

Searches using an estimated 1840 or 1841 birth year surfaced three additional records whose original images were downloaded and reviewed:

- record 636983: Aurora Collazo, age 99, born in Utuado and died in Santurce in 1942
- record 450755: Aurora Rodríguez, a child born about 1943 and died in Bayamón in 1945
- record 861212: Aurora Santiago Santiago, born 22 February 1944 and died four days later in Loíza

All are rejected. Ancestry's birth-year matching is relevance-weighted and returned records with plainly incompatible ages, so this portion is not treated as a complete cohort screen.

## Conclusion

No defensible death record for Aurora Pérez was found. The exact Naguabo year-by-year screen is a bounded negative at the derivative-index level, not proof that she did not die there. Plausible explanations include an omitted or mistranscribed index entry, a substantially different recorded name, death outside Naguabo, survival beyond 1950, or a missing civil record.
