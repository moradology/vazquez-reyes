# Máximo Vázquez and Josefa Rivera: neighboring-parish sweep

Date: 25 July 2026

## Question

What can neighboring parish material add to the documented family of Máximo
Vázquez and Josefa Rivera, and can it narrow their deaths without forcing weak
same-name matches?

## New child candidate

The complete San Lorenzo baptism transcription contains this exact row:

- baptized: María Josefa Vázquez
- father: Máximo Vázquez
- mother: Josefa de Rivera
- baptism: 12 March 1826
- overall image: 795
- item image: 281
- folio: 84

The exact parent pair is highly specific and makes María Josefa a strong
candidate child of the direct couple and sister of Atilano. The same
transcription set also recovers the already confirmed Miguel de los Santos
Vázquez entry of 15 July 1824, which is a useful internal check.

The new child is **not confirmed yet**. Both available browser sessions stopped
at FamilySearch sign-in when opening the original frame. Until the original is
captured and read, we do not know whether the register adds a birth date,
legitimacy or classification language, residence, or godparents, and we do not
promote the relationship beyond a strong transcription candidate.

All four public baptism transcription exports were preserved locally with
SHA-256 hashes. The target row is in `transcription-c.csv`.

## Latest likely living evidence

Atilano Vázquez and Juana Rodríguez married in Yabucoa on 10 February 1849.
The original entry explicitly calls Juana's father Andrés deceased. It names
Atilano's parents, Máximo Vázquez and Josefa de Rivera, without a deceased
label.

That within-entry contrast cautiously supports that the priest treated Máximo
and Josefa as living in February 1849. It is not direct proof: a clerk can omit
a status word, and this is neither a residence nor a death record. The ledger
therefore records a latest-*likely*-living boundary, not a confirmed alive-on
date.

## Burial screening

The complete San Lorenzo burial transcription for 1856–1867 contains 2,447
data rows. It was screened for Máximo or Maximino Vázquez and Josefa Rivera or
Ribera. The superficially similar entries are different people:

- Josefa de Rivera, age 10, in 1856
- Máximo Vázquez, age 22, son of Petrona Vázquez, in 1865
- Maximino Vázquez, age 5, son of Elena Fernández, in 1866

No defensible entry for the direct couple was found. This is a bounded
transcription-level negative, not proof of absence and not a substitute for
original-image review.

The San Lorenzo church-book inventory identifies a major gap: burial books for
1829–1856 are missing from the published transcription sequence. That gap
overlaps the most useful period immediately after the couple's last known
children and the 1849 contextual boundary.

Neighboring published inventories keep several exact next targets open:

1. Yabucoa burial material covering 1835–1869, although the books are described
   as fragmented and out of order.
2. Juncos burial volumes covering 1826–1843 and 1844–1856.
3. Las Piedras and Juncos combined burial material for earlier jurisdictional
   overlap.
4. Duplicate or diocesan copies for San Lorenzo's missing 1829–1856 span.

## Same-name rejection

A Cayey reconstruction contains another Máximo Vázquez. The complete Cayey
transcriptions identify that household with parents Mateo Vázquez and
Victoriana Sánchez and wives María Josefa Ortiz and Juana Vázquez.

The direct Máximo's original 1805 marriage instead names parents Francisco and
María Cortez and wife Josefa Ribera. The direct couple then appears across
Humacao, Juncos, San Lorenzo, and Yabucoa records.

These are distinct households. The Cayey person is explicitly rejected as a
merge candidate unless a future original record supplies an identity bridge
that also resolves the incompatible parents and spouses.
