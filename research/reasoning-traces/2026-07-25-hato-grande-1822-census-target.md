# Hato Grande 1822 nominal census target

Date: 25 July 2026

## Question

Can an early household record place Máximo Vázquez, Josefa Rivera, and their
children together between the documented 1819 Juncos baptism of Juan de Dios
and the documented 1824 San Lorenzo baptism of Miguel de los Santos?

## Exact household source identified

The Hijos de Coamo early-census inventory identifies an **1822 Hato Grande
(San Lorenzo) “Censo Nominal de los vecinos,” indexed by first name**. Its link
resolves to this exact FamilySearch frame:

- ARK: `3Q9M-CSV2-TQM9-J`
- film-browser index: `i=1629`
- displayed overall image: 1630
- catalog: 607211

This is the highest-value current household target. Hato Grande is the earlier
name of San Lorenzo, where Máximo and Josefa had a documented son in 1824 and
where a complete baptism transcription places another probable daughter in
1826. The 1822 census falls directly between those parish events.

The finding aid identifies the source and frame; it does **not** name Máximo,
Josefa, or a child. FamilySearch currently redirects the exact frame to sign-in,
so the original has not yet been captured or read.

## 1817 militia material

A separate 362-page compiled transcription on Scribd includes a Hato Grande
urban-militia roster dated 25 July 1817. The publicly surfaced excerpt describes
three companies and separates the historical categories “blancos” and
“morenos.” The visible first-company excerpt contains two other men named
Máximo—Máximo López and Máximo González Cardosa—not Máximo Vázquez.

This material is retained only as a lead:

- it is a militia roster of men, not a household census;
- it cannot identify Josefa or the children;
- the complete Hato Grande section and original AGPR images have not been
  captured and reviewed;
- a missing name would not prove that a person was absent from Hato Grande.

No identity or origin conclusion is drawn from the militia excerpt.

## Result

- No new ancestor or pre-Puerto Rico origin was established.
- The exact 1822 household source and frame are now pinned.
- The 1817 roster was classified correctly as an incomplete collateral lead,
  not negative census evidence.
