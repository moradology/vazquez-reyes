# Record-image capture policy

Every image relevant to a research question is retained locally, including
candidate records that are later rejected or not cited in a conclusion.

Images belong under the Git-ignored `research/pulls/images/` directory. They
must not be committed merely because they were reviewed.

For each image, preserve:

- the full page or full document;
- a readable crop of the relevant household or entry when useful;
- the source URL and source-registry ID;
- capture date;
- people and research cases involved;
- disposition: `used`, `not_used`, `rejected`, or `pending`;
- a short explanation of the disposition.

When the host or record rights do not permit republication, the capture remains
in the private local archive. Public-domain or otherwise publishable excerpts
may be added to the family site only after a separate privacy and rights review.

Search-result thumbnails and index summaries do not replace the underlying
record image when an image exists.
